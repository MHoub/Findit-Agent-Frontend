Short answer: Your archive documents at least 60 distinct TV reports of yours involving Alexander Kraus (also credited as Krause, occasionally misspelled “Alexader Kraus” or written as “alexanderkraus”). The collaboration is documented from at least May 2015 through 2026. His role was almost always editing (cutter); on one Europamagazin report he was the cameraman.

This is a documented minimum. Completed reports or reports with a verifiable credit were counted after deduplicating drafts, copies and organizational files for the same production. Requests for a particular editor without a final editing credit were excluded.
