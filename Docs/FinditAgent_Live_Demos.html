Live Research Demos

Real research sessions. An existing archive. Different questions and AI models.

These demonstrations show Findit6Agent working with my personal archive of roughly 35 years of journalism: tens of thousands of files, with no prior indexing, embeddings or specially prepared demo collection. Findit6Agent performs all local file operations.

Two approaches to research: In Agent Mode, capable cloud-based LLMs such as Claude, GPT and Grok can plan complex searches, decide on a strategy, and revise it as they go. In Guided Mode, Findit6Agent breaks the task into predefined steps (chunks). Smaller locally running models handle focused sub-tasks such as interpreting the question, examining aspects, evaluating retrieved documents and synthesizing an answer. The workflow is defined in the program rather than planned autonomously by the model.

All demonstrations use the same real archive, accumulated over 35 years of work as a German television journalist. Naturally, the questions were chosen because this archive contains relevant information. These are examples, not a specially prepared dataset: a historian, researcher, lawyer or business owner might ask entirely different questions about their own files. The subject changes, but the principle is the same. Results depend on the material, the model and the question 鈥?imagine what you could investigate in your own archive.

How can a small local AI model find a substance it has not identified yet? Unlike powerful cloud models that can independently plan and pursue complex research, Guided Mode gives smaller models a predefined sequence of manageable steps. This demonstration makes those steps visible: the question is separated into aspects, translated into AND/OR conditions, optimized with truncation and expanded with alternative terms before Findit6 searches the local archive.

The question revisits the SF6 topic investigated by GPT Sol in video 03. Here, Qwen3 14B running through Ollama starts without naming SF6 in the search expression, then learns the answer from documents found by Findit6.
